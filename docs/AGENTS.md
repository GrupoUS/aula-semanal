# docs/ — AGENTS.md

Este subtree owns project history, operational runbooks, plans, visual references, and integration notes; it is not a source of product facts.

## Authorities

- Product claims and offers come from the root `PRODUCT.md`.
- Visual direction and assets come from the root `DESIGN.md` and the tracked Figma exports.
- Review gates and rejection criteria come from the root `REVIEW.md`.
- Lead operations are documented in `planilha-leads.md` and `operacao-leads-aula-semanal.md`, while implementation status is tracked in `aula-semanal-implementacao.md`.

## Rules

- Treat dated analyses, plans, and inherited documents as historical context unless a root authority confirms the fact.
- Do not add secrets, production credentials, or raw lead PII to documentation or screenshots.
- Update operational docs when an approved integration or gate contract changes; do not redesign product or visual direction here.
- Keep Figma and logo exports referenced by the implementation; do not replace them with external downloads during setup.
